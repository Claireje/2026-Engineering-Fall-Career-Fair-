WHAT TO EXPECT

Guidelines & Dress Code

These events are designed to encourage casual, open conversation. Here's how to make the most of your night.

Before You Go

RSVP Required

You must RSVP to attend. RSVPs help us plan the atrium layout and ensure adequate refreshments for everyone. RSVP here.

Attend the Right Night

Sunday's reception features companies attending the Monday Career Fair. Monday's reception features companies attending the Tuesday Career Fair. Check the company list to plan accordingly.

At the Reception

No Resumes

Leave your resume at home. Receptions are intentionally resume-free to encourage genuine, low-pressure conversation with recruiters.

Bring Your MCard

You'll need your MCard to enter the reception, so make sure to bring it with you.

Business Casual Attire

Dress professionally but comfortably. Business casual is recommended to maintain a polished yet relaxed atmosphere.
